The Studio

Terminal Software is a remote, independent software solutions studio. Founded by Charles Bass, the studio operates at the intersection of quantitative data analysis and interactive systems design.

Whether we are architecting high-frequency data pipelines for algorithmic traders or designing complex economic engines for our management simulation games, the philosophy remains the same: Simulation First.

We believe in deep, reactive systems. We believe in providing developers with clean, un-bloated APIs. We believe in premium gaming experiences free from microtransaction paywalls and artificial timers.
