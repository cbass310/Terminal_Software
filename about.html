Our Story

Terminal Software is a remote, independent game studio operating out of California. Founded by Charles Alfred Bass II, the studio was born out of a simple frustration: a lack of truly deep, strategy-first sports management games on the mobile market.

We believe that the most compelling drama in sports doesn't just happen on the field, the court, or in the ring—it happens in the executive suite. It happens during contract negotiations, in the booking meetings, and when fighting for television ratings.

As a solopreneur studio, Terminal Software is agile and deeply connected to the communities we serve. Our flagship title, Squared Circle Tycoon, is a love letter to the business of professional wrestling, meticulously designed for fans who want to trade arcade button-mashing for complex economic strategy, roster management, and long-term storytelling.

No microtransaction paywalls. No timers. Just you, the data, and the dynasty you build.
