How EV Works on {{SPORTSBOOK}}

Expected Value (EV) represents the average amount of money you can expect to win or lose per bet if you placed the exact same wager on {{SPORTSBOOK}} infinite times. If a bet has a positive Expected Value (+EV), it means the odds {{SPORTSBOOK}} is offering are better than the actual probability of the event occurring.

Professional bettors do not pick winners. They pick +EV lines. Over a large enough sample size, variance diminishes, and mathematical edge equals profit.
