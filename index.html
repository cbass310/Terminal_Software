STEP INTO THE EXECUTIVE SUITE

Welcome to the boardroom. The spotlights are blinding, the egos are bigger than the pyrotechnics, and the only thing that matters is the bottom line. In Squared Circle Tycoon, you’re not just a fan—you’re the boss. The power to build a wrestling empire from a local high school gym to a global phenomenon is in your hands.

Every decision rests on your shoulders. Who gets the title shot? Which rivalry will headline the next Pay-Per-View? How will you handle a main event star’s contract demands? This is a cutthroat world of strategic booking, financial warfare, and locker room politics. Your legacy is defined not by the matches, but by the empire you build.

BUILD YOUR DYNASTY, ONE MATCH AT A TIME

🎟️

Live Booking Engine

You are the booker. Craft every show from the opening match to the epic main event. Watch your booking decisions translate into real-time match ratings and fan feedback.

💰

Dynamic Economy

The war for ratings is fought with cash. Negotiate crucial weekly TV deals, sign lucrative ring sponsorships, and manage your budget to upgrade your venue from a bingo hall to a sold-out stadium.

👥

Roster Management

Draft your dream roster from a pool of over 80 unique wrestlers. Navigate the treacherous waters of contract negotiations, backstage politics, star-making pushes, and career-threatening injuries.

🔥

Rivalry & Heat

Great wrestling is built on great stories. Meticulously build animosity and history between your top stars. The more 'heat' a rivalry has, the bigger the box office payoff for your Pay-Per-Views.

📱

Social Feed Feedback

The fans and the critics are watching your every move. After every show, check the dynamic 'Dirt Sheet' and social media feeds to see the immediate fallout from your booking decisions and storylines.
