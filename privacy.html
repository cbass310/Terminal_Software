Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 6, 2026

Terminal Software ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by Terminal Software when you use our website and our mobile applications, including Squared Circle Tycoon.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information about you if we have a reason to do so—for example, to provide our services, to communicate with you, or to make our games better.

  • Information you provide directly: When you contact us for support or business inquiries, we may collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Game Data (Future Updates): Currently, Squared Circle Tycoon operates primarily offline. However, as we introduce online features such as global leaderboards, we may collect in-game usernames, high scores, and gameplay statistics necessary to maintain those services.
  • Automatically Collected Information: We may collect standard crash reports and analytics data provided by mobile app storefronts (like Apple or Google) to help us identify bugs and improve performance. This data is generally aggregated and anonymized.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our games and services.
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests.
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with our games.
  • Operate and maintain online features, such as leaderboards (when implemented).

3. Sharing Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our services (e.g., hosting online leaderboards or analytics). These third parties have access to your personal data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your personal information, and we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure.

5. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
